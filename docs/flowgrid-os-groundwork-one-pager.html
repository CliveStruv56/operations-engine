The registers behind the reports

  • Funding. Sources, amounts sought and secured, drawdowns, and a programme catalogue that flags when a funder's terms were last verified.
  • Budget. Line-by-line budget, forecast and actual, with variance calculated rather than typed.
  • Risk. Likelihood, impact, owner, mitigation and review date.
  • Planning conditions. Pre-commencement flags, discharge status and dates.
  • Stakeholders & tasks. Who holds what, milestones, and statutory-tagged obligations.

Why the drafts are trustworthy

  • Figures come from rows, never the model. Budget and funding tables render from your records; the AI writes the narrative around them.
  • Gaps are marked, not filled. Missing facts appear as [TO CONFIRM] rather than a plausible invention.
  • Claims from your documents cite the page they came from, resolvable back to the source.
  • Draft-first, always. Every document lands watermarked DRAFT with a human review step. Nothing sends itself.
  • Stale programme terms warn on page one of any bid built on them.

Who it's for

  • Development consultants and enablers running 3–15 concurrent community-led schemes — CLTs, cohousing groups, community buildings.
  • Community Land Trusts and housing co-ops managing their own scheme and reporting to a board.
  • Consultancies serving several clients: one branded workspace per client, each one sealed off from the others at the database, with your practice's name on it rather than ours.

Pricing. Modules switch on per client workspace — there is no separate module licence. Core is £29/seat/month, Pro £49, and Managed £799/month for consultancies who want the workspaces run for them.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No Gantt chart. Milestones and dates, not a scheduling tool competing with the one you already have.
  • No development appraisal engine. A flat budget table with variance — appraisals stay in your spreadsheet, which is better at them.
  • No community-facing portal. This is consultant-facing; what the client sees is the document you send them.
  • No automatic submissions. Nothing is filed, sent or lodged on your behalf.

Narrow on purpose. The module earns its place on one loop — spine current, report drafts itself — rather than by replacing tools that already work.
