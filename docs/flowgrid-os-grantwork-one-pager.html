What it keeps hold of

  • Funders and a seeded UK catalogue — lottery funds, trusts, community foundations — each row carrying the date its criteria were last verified.
  • Applications through the pipeline, with amounts requested and awarded, restricted or unrestricted.
  • Award conditions — including which must be discharged before you can draw down.
  • Impact measures with baseline, target and unit, and the outcomes recorded against each reporting period.
  • The obligation calendar — every reporting period, its due date, and what is overdue.

Numbers a funder can trust

  • Every figure comes from a recorded outcome. The impact table renders from your rows; the AI writes only the narrative around it.
  • An unmeasured outcome says "not recorded" — never a zero, and never a plausible estimate. Absence must not read as failure, or as success.
  • Over-delivery is shown as achieved, uncapped, rather than quietly rounded to target.
  • Unverified funder criteria warn on page one of any bid built on them.
  • Draft-first with a DRAFT watermark. Nothing reaches a funder without a human sending it.

Who it's for

  • Charities, CICs and community organisations with roughly £100k–£3m income, running a rolling portfolio of applications and the reporting that follows.
  • Fundraising consultancies and freelance bid writers — one branded workspace per client, each sealed off from the others at the database, carrying your name rather than ours.
  • Infrastructure bodies — CVSs, community foundations and networks supporting many small organisations at once.

Pricing. Modules switch on per client workspace — no separate module licence. Core is £29/seat/month direct; Managed at £799/month suits consultancies running workspaces on behalf of several charities.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No donor fundraising. Individual giving, direct debits and Gift Aid stay in your CRM — this is the institutional grant side only.
  • No restricted-fund accounting. It records what was awarded and reported, not your ledger.
  • No funder portal or auto-submission. Returns are drafted for a human to send, never filed for you.
  • No impact framework beyond measures against targets. Deliberately simple, because the figures have to be defensible to a funder.

Narrow on purpose. One loop — application current, return assembles itself — done properly, rather than a second CRM.
