Design rationale

What changed, and the evidence behind it

01

A greeting, not a void

The empty state becomes onboarding: a vault status chip, a serif welcome, and four starter prompts drawn from your documents — the pattern NN/g and the Shape of AI library document as the fix for blank-canvas abandonment. One chip is tagged with the mode it triggers, so modes get discovered by doing.

02

Modes you can see

The six modes leave the native dropdown and become a segmented pill row on the composer — Perplexity’s pattern. The active mode is filled terracotta; switching it re-labels the placeholder text, so the input always says what it will do.

03

Trust has its own colour

Sage green appears only on grounded states — the vault chip, the vault toggle, citation badges. Borrowed from Intercom’s scoped Fin orange: one glance separates “from your documents” from ordinary chrome.

04

Two voices, one system

Fraunces speaks only in greetings and section titles — the warm moment. Everything functional is Plus Jakarta Sans on a 12 / 14 / 16 scale, and every grey has been verified ≥ 4.5:1 on every surface it touches. The mono small-caps are retired.

05

A sidebar with a spine

Search first (⌘K). Nav descriptions dropped — the “128 docs” badge explains Vault better than a sentence. Chats group under “Today” with hover-revealed pin/rename. The trial gets a proper card with a progress bar; the footer no longer overlaps itself.

06

Depth from surfaces, not shadows

Notion’s move: white cards floating on warm cream create hierarchy with almost no shadow. The single soft shadow is spent where focus belongs — the composer. The orphaned icon rail is now a labelled top bar with tooltips.
