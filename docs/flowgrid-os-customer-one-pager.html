Stop stitching together chat, document, slide and research tools. Flowgrid OS gives your team a single workspace where answers cite your own files, each task goes to the most cost-effective AI model, and usage never surprises you.

Why it matters

  • One bill, one login. Consolidate chat, vault, research, slides and projects into one workspace.
  • Your name on it. Your logo, colours and house style are set up from day one, so it feels like your own tool.
  • No single-supplier dependence. We match each task to the right AI model, so you get top-quality results without paying premium prices for simple jobs.

Built for privacy

  • Your data stays yours. It is walled off from every other organisation, enforced in the database itself, not just in the software.
  • UK data residency, no training on your files. Your data is held in the UK, our AI providers do not keep what you send them, and nothing is used to train AI models.
  • UK GDPR and a DPA. We provide a Data Processing Agreement, and every action in your workspace is logged.
