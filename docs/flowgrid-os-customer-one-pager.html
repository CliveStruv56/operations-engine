Turn what your organisation knows into work you can trust.

Find cited answers, keep recurring work structured, and produce outputs ready for review. Flowgrid OS connects your source documents, confirmed facts and live projects in one workspace, and every output cites its source.

Who it's for. UK small organisations and specialist teams (SMEs, charities and development trusts) managing recurring bids, reporting, development projects and evidence-heavy work. No technical team needed.

Why it matters

  • One bill, one login. Consolidate chat, vault, research, slides and projects into one workspace.
  • Your name on it. Your logo, colours and house style are set up from day one, so it feels like your own tool.
  • You review everything. Flowgrid never sends a document on your behalf. You check the citations, then you send.
  • No single-supplier dependence. We match each task to the right AI model, so you get top-quality results without paying premium prices for simple jobs.

Built for privacy

  • Your data stays yours. Your workspace is isolated from every other customer at the database level, not only by application code.
  • Zero-data-retention hosting. AI requests go through one gateway to vetted providers with zero-data-retention hosting by default, and your documents are not used to train anyone's models.
  • UK GDPR, a DPA and an easy exit. A UK GDPR Data Processing Agreement is part of your terms and names where your data is hosted. Export in standard formats (PDF, PowerPoint, CSV), with return and deletion agreed up front.
